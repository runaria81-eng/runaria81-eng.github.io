How to play

Fishing Cat (Call the Cat 5) · Updated: 21 September 2026

This game is not about mashing. The whole decision is strike or let it pass. Everything you need is below.

1. What to tap

Three fish show large ripples, each on its own rhythm. Tap a fish's body the moment its ripple meets the inner dotted outline. Water, labels and bars are not targets. On a keyboard, 1, 2 and 3 each aim at one fish.

2. The two bars

Green is catch progress; amber is alert. Every hit raises progress, but it raises alert too. When alert runs high, let one ring pass and work on another fish. A perfect landed after resting earns extra progress.

3. How fish escape

A fish you have never touched never flees. Neglect is counted only after your first hit: four ignored rings in a row and it escapes. A successful hit resets neglect, but mistimed taps still raise alert, and a full alert bar also causes an escape. An empty spot refills after 2.2 seconds with a fresh, untouched fish. Three escapes end the run.

4. Relay

Land a successful hit within 4.2 seconds to keep the relay going. Deliberately letting a ripple pass does not break it; a miss, an escape or the timer running out does. Points from a successful hit are saved immediately.

5. Scales and the shop

Catching fish earns scales. The in-game shop is bought with scales only — there is no real-money purchase. The Cushion boosts starting progress, the Bell calms alert on rests, the Net preserves progress on rests, and the Lure raises rare spawn weights. Gear you own applies to every fish automatically.

6. Field guide and saving

Each species you catch is recorded with its largest and smallest size, and filling the guide opens new ponds. Score, guide entries and scales are saved the instant you land a catch. Progress is stored on this device only; there is no account and no cloud save.

7. Timing calibration

Input and output latency differs by device. On first run you tap eight times to measure it, and you can redo it any time from Settings. If the timing feels off, start there.
