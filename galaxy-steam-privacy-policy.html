Steam Privacy Policy

Call the Cat: Galaxy · Operator: Baren81 · Updated August 1, 2026

The Steam build is intended not to include AdMob rewarded ads or mobile advertising SDKs.

1. Data That May Be Processed

The game may store progress, settings, resources, and unlocks locally. When Steam features are enabled, Valve may process Steam ID, ownership, play activity, achievements, Steam Cloud saves, purchase or refund results, and platform diagnostics.

2. Purposes

Data may be used to run the game, save and restore progress, verify ownership, support achievements and Steam Cloud, diagnose problems, support purchases or refunds, and improve stability.

3. Valve and Steam

Valve controls Steam account and platform data under its Privacy Policy. Baren81 does not directly process card or bank details.

4. Analytics and Ads

The Steam build does not show mobile rewarded ads. Steam or the operating system may process platform-level usage, performance, and crash information under its settings and policies. This policy will be updated before any separate analytics or diagnostic SDK is enabled.

5. Retention, Deletion, and Transfers

Local data may remain until reset, deletion, or uninstall. Steam Cloud, achievements, ownership, and purchase records follow Valve's retention rules and account settings. Valve may process data internationally under its safeguards.

6. User Rights

Steam-controlled data can be managed through Steam privacy settings. Questions about information directly held by Baren81 may be sent below.

7. Children, Security, and Changes

Baren81 does not knowingly request direct identifiers such as names or contact details in the game. Reasonable safeguards are applied, and this page will be updated when features or legal requirements change.
