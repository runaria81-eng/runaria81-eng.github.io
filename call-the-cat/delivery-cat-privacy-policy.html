Privacy Policy

Call the Cat: Delivery
Last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy applies to the web game, including the version hosted on CrazyGames, and to the versions distributed through Google Play and Apple App Store. Platform providers may separately process information under their own policies.

1. Operator and contact

Operator: Baren81. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to barendevlog@gmail.com.

2. Information handled by the operator

Game progress, language, and sound settings are stored locally in the browser or app on the device first. In the Google Play build, when you connect Google Play Games, Google Play Games processes your Play Games identifier and a Saved Games snapshot of the game progress so it can be synced and restored. The operator runs no account server of its own and does not collect a Delivery Cat account, name, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location. Online ranking profiles are not provided.

The map and destinations are game presentation only, not real-time location tracking or navigation. If you email the operator, the email address and message are used to answer the request.

3. The CrazyGames version

On CrazyGames the game runs inside crazygames.com and saves progress through the CrazyGames SDK data module. For a guest the save stays in the browser's own storage. For a player signed in to a CrazyGames account, CrazyGames keeps the save on that account and syncs it across the devices where the player opens the game. The save holds game values only, such as money, day, energy, equipment, and settings.

The operator does not receive a CrazyGames account name, email address, or sign-in credentials, and does not operate an account server for the game. CrazyGames separately processes site usage, account, cookie, consent, and advertising information as the operator of crazygames.com and as the publisher of aggregate play statistics shown to developers, such as play counts, session length, and return rate. Those are handled under the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics

The Google Play and Apple App Store builds use the native Firebase Analytics SDK to send two custom game events: delivery completed and rewarded-ad reward granted. Each event carries only an event count; the game does not send a player account ID, name, email, save contents, or GPS location. Firebase may also automatically process basic app-usage and technical information required by its Analytics SDK. The web builds, including the CrazyGames version, do not use Firebase Analytics.

5. Advertising and platform processing

A top banner advertisement and optional rewarded ads in Google Play and Apple App Store builds use the native AdMob SDK. AdMob or the platform may process an ad request, device/app identifiers, ad interaction, diagnostics, and an IP-derived approximate location to provide and measure the ad.

The CrazyGames version currently shows no advertising and contains no AdMob or other third-party ad code. If advertising is enabled there at a later stage, it will be served only through the CrazyGames SDK; CrazyGames and its advertising partners may then process the ad request, device and browser identifiers, ad interaction, diagnostics, and an IP-derived approximate location to provide and measure the ad, and consent for that advertising is collected by CrazyGames on its own site. This page will be updated on the date that happens.

The operator does not use precise location or a Delivery Cat account server. Advertising and platform records are retained and deleted according to the relevant provider's policy.

6. Payments

The game has no in-app purchase, Google Play Billing flow, paid product, or ad-removal product. No build provides a checkout, purchase restoration, or refund flow. No payment method, card, or bank details are requested or received by the operator.

7. Storage, deletion, and requests

Local data can be replaced through the in-game New game flow or removed through browser/app storage controls. On CrazyGames, a save kept on a CrazyGames account is managed through that account. Operator-held inquiry information is kept only as long as needed to handle the request, unless a longer period is required by law.

For a request about information held by the operator, contact barendevlog@gmail.com. Requests about Firebase, AdMob, Google Play, Apple, or CrazyGames records must be made through the relevant provider's privacy controls.

8. Children, security, and changes

The game does not create a Delivery Cat server account for a child. Age ratings, parental controls, consent, and child-safety requirements follow the target platform and applicable law. We update this page when the data flow or platform services change and show the updated date.

3. CrazyGames 버전

CrazyGames 버전은 crazygames.com 안에서 실행되며 CrazyGames SDK의 데이터 모듈로 진행을 저장합니다. 게스트로 플레이하면 저장은 해당 브라우저 저장소에만 남습니다. CrazyGames 계정으로 로그인한 경우에는 CrazyGames가 그 계정에 저장 데이터를 보관하고 플레이어가 게임을 여는 다른 기기에도 동기화합니다. 저장 데이터에는 소지금, 날짜, 체력, 장비, 설정과 같은 게임 값만 담깁니다.

운영자는 CrazyGames 계정 이름·이메일 주소·로그인 정보를 받지 않으며 게임용 계정 서버를 운영하지 않습니다. CrazyGames는 crazygames.com의 운영자로서 사이트 이용, 계정, 쿠키, 동의, 광고 정보를 별도로 처리하고, 플레이 횟수·플레이 시간·재방문율 같은 집계 지표를 개발자에게 제공합니다. 이는 CrazyGames 개인정보 처리방침에 따릅니다.

4. 분석

Google Play와 Apple App Store 버전은 네이티브 Firebase Analytics SDK로 배달 완료·보상형 광고 보상 지급의 두 가지 맞춤 게임 이벤트를 보냅니다. 각 이벤트에는 횟수만 담기며, 플레이어 계정 ID·이름·이메일·저장 내용·GPS 위치는 보내지 않습니다. Firebase Analytics SDK가 기본 앱 사용 정보와 기술 정보를 자동으로 처리할 수 있다는 점은 별도입니다. CrazyGames 버전을 포함한 웹 버전은 Firebase Analytics를 사용하지 않습니다.

5. 광고와 플랫폼 처리

Google Play와 Apple App Store 버전의 화면 상단 배너 광고와 선택형 보상형 광고는 네이티브 AdMob SDK를 사용합니다. 광고 제공과 측정을 위해 AdMob 또는 플랫폼이 광고 요청, 기기·앱 식별자, 광고 상호작용, 진단 정보와 IP에서 추정한 대략적인 위치를 처리할 수 있습니다.

CrazyGames 버전은 현재 광고를 표시하지 않으며 AdMob을 비롯한 외부 광고 코드가 들어 있지 않습니다. 이후 이 버전에서 광고를 켜는 경우 광고는 CrazyGames SDK를 통해서만 제공되며, 그때 CrazyGames와 광고 파트너가 광고 제공·측정을 위해 광고 요청, 기기·브라우저 식별자, 광고 상호작용, 진단 정보와 IP에서 추정한 대략적인 위치를 처리할 수 있습니다. 해당 광고에 대한 동의는 CrazyGames가 자체 사이트에서 받습니다. 그 시점에 이 페이지를 갱신합니다.

운영자는 정확한 위치나 Delivery Cat 계정 서버를 사용하지 않습니다. 광고와 플랫폼 기록은 해당 제공자의 정책에 따라 보관·삭제됩니다.
