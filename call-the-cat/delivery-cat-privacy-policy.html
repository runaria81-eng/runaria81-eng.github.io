Call the Cat 4 · Delivery Cat
Last updated: August 30, 2026 · Release 0.1.0

This policy describes the information handled by the current Delivery Cat release. It applies to the web game and versions distributed through Google Play, Apple App Store, and Apps-in-Toss. Platform providers may separately process information under their own policies.

한국어 개인정보 처리방침 Terms of Use

1. Operator and contact

Operator name used for this game: Baren81 · runaria81. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to rurununky@naver.com.

2. Information handled by release 0.1.0

This release does not ask you to create a Delivery Cat account and does not directly request your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, precise GPS location, payment-card details, or advertising identifier.

To restore play on the same browser or app web view, the game stores gameplay state and preferences locally. This can include delivery progress, virtual wallet and item counts, selected city and home, language, sound settings, and map-label preference. The current game has no operator server or cloud-save profile.

Release 0.1.0 has no active analytics SDK, live AdMob ad request, live Apps-in-Toss ad request, or in-app purchase flow. The existence of a platform SDK dependency or a registered console app does not mean that data collection or advertising is active in this release.

3. Why local data is used

Local game data is used only to continue the game, restore the selected settings, and provide the requested game flow on the same browser or app web view. It is not used to identify you by name, build an online profile, or operate an online ranking.

4. Platform providers

Google Play, Apple App Store, and Apps-in-Toss may process account, device, installation, billing, security, or usage information as part of their own platform services. That processing is controlled by the relevant provider and its privacy policy. When a future platform feature sends limited information to the game operator, the applicable release policy will identify the data, purpose, provider, retention, and user controls before activation.

5. Future advertising, purchases, and services

A later mobile or Apps-in-Toss release may add an optional rewarded advertisement for a 1.5× travel-speed effect lasting 20 game minutes. If Google AdMob or another advertising service is activated, its data categories, consent choices, purposes, sharing, retention, and controls will be added to this policy before the change is released. Release 0.1.0 does not make that collection.

Login, cloud saves, analytics, precise location, diagnostics, or in-app purchases are also future possibilities only. They are not enabled by this policy. A release that adds one of them must update this policy and the relevant store or Apps-in-Toss declarations first.

6. Storage, deletion, and requests

Local game data remains until you clear the browser or app storage, remove the app, or use the game's new-game flow. Because this release does not create an operator account or cloud profile, Baren81 cannot restore a deleted local save from a server. Platform-controlled account, billing, advertising, or diagnostic records must be managed through the relevant Google, Apple, or Toss controls.

You may email a privacy request to rurununky@naver.com. We will explain whether Baren81 holds information covered by the request and what action is available under applicable law. Requests may require enough information to verify the requester.

7. Sharing and international processing

Baren81 does not sell your name or contact information. The current release does not send a game profile to an operator server. Hosting, browser, operating-system, app-store, and platform services may process technical information under their own policies, including outside your country or region.

8. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly ask children to submit names or contact details to Baren81. Age ratings, parental controls, consent, and child-safety requirements are determined according to the target platform and applicable law.

9. Changes

We will update this page when the game's data flow, platform services, or legal requirements change. The updated page will show a new revision date. The current effective date is August 30, 2026.
