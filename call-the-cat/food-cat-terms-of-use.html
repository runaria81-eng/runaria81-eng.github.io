Terms of Use

Food Cat · Dodo’s Fish Delivery
Updated: September 5, 2026

These Terms apply to the Food Cat web game and future versions distributed through Apps-in-Toss, Google Play, and Apple App Store.

1. What the game is

Food Cat is a fictional balance-and-delivery game. Scores, stages, fish, rewards, and game time are game values only. The game is not a real food-delivery, employment, or transportation service.

2. Access and local saves

No Food Cat account is required. Progress, language, and sound preferences are stored locally in the browser or app on the device. Clearing storage, removing the app, changing devices, or starting a new game can remove unsynced progress. Online rankings are not currently provided.

3. Advertising and payments

Food Cat may offer optional rewarded ads and banner ads in supported release versions. Watching an ad is never required to play. If advertising or payment features are added, the in-game notice and Privacy Policy will be updated before release. A failed ad, consent refusal, or network failure must not corrupt local progress.

4. Fair use

You must not bypass safeguards, manipulate local saves or scores, use bots or automation for an unfair advantage, interfere with other players, or abuse a bug.

Unless stated otherwise, Food Cat code, writing, interface, original characters, art, and audio are owned by or licensed to Baren81. Fonts, music, and other third-party materials remain subject to their own licences and the release credits.

6. Changes and contact

We may change balance, content, supported devices, or availability to fix errors, improve safety, or meet platform requirements. Questions may be sent to barendevlog@gmail.com.
