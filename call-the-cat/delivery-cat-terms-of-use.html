These Terms apply to the web game and versions distributed through Google Play and Apple App Store. The platform's own terms and rules also apply to distribution, accounts, advertising, and testing.

1. What the game is

Call the Cat: Delivery Cat is a fictional cat-courier simulation. Wallet balances, delivery fees, food prices, homes, routes, time, energy, ranks, and rewards are game values only. The game is not a real food-delivery, navigation, courier-employment, or transportation service.

2. Access and local saves

The game does not require a Delivery Cat account. Progress, language, and settings are stored locally in the browser or app on the device first. In the Google Play build you may connect Google Play Games, and the game then syncs progress through Saved Games so it can be restored after a reinstall or on another device. The web and Apple App Store builds keep progress on the device only. Clearing storage, removing the app, changing devices, or starting a new game may remove progress that has not been synced. Online rankings are not provided.

3. Advertising and payments

The Google Play build sells one non-consumable in-app product through Google Play Billing: a permanent 2× travel speed unlock. The Apple App Store build and the web build do not offer it. Google Play and Apple App Store versions may also offer an optional rewarded ad. After successful completion of the ad, the game grants the reward shown on screen.

Google Play handles payment, purchase history and refunds; the operator never receives card or bank details. Refund requests go through Google Play's own refund process. Once a refund completes, the game rechecks purchase state on launch and on resume, turns the permanent 2× off, and the rewarded-ad and in-game speed boosts remain available. A failed ad, consent refusal or payment failure must not corrupt a save or grant a paid entitlement.

Ads are not required to play. Consent, region, network, device, or platform conditions may make an ad unavailable. A failed ad, consent refusal, or network failure must not corrupt local game progress.
