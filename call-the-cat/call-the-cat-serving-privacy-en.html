1. Scope and operator

baren81 explains how information is used by the current web version of Call the Cat: Serving! and its legal pages. External websites have their own privacy policies.

2. Information, purposes and retention

Progress (including stage, score, time remaining and saved gameplay state) and high scores are stored in your browser to restore play and display records. Language and audio preferences are stored to apply your settings. They remain until deleted or replaced. If you email support, your email address, message and attachments are used to answer and resolve the request, retained only as needed and deleted when no longer necessary. Records subject to a legal retention obligation are kept separately for the required period.

3. Information not requested

The current game has no account server, cloud saving or online leaderboard. It does not request your name, phone number, address, contacts, photos or precise location. Local game saves are not transmitted to an operator server. Hosting providers may separately process technical access records.

4. Local storage and deletion

The game uses browser localStorage. Clear this game's site data in your browser privacy settings to remove progress, high scores, language and audio preferences. New Game replaces a saved run, but does not clear every preference. Clear each browser separately. There is no server backup, so the operator cannot restore deleted progress.

5. Advertising, analytics and payments

No live advertising, analytics or payment SDK is connected to the current default web game. The game therefore does not send advertising IDs or purchases to those services. Before introducing these features, the applicable version will disclose actual information, purposes, providers and any required consent.

6. External pages and hosting

GitHub Pages records visitor IP addresses for security when you access its hosted legal pages. This is separate from local game saves. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Pages documentation. Other hosting or distribution platforms may process access records to provide and secure their services.

7. Sharing and overseas processing

The operator does not sell local game records or share them for advertising. The game currently has no function that transmits local saves to an outsourced processor. External pages and email services may process information on their providers' infrastructure. Consult those providers' policies for locations and retention.

8. Your rights and children

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of information you emailed to the operator. Only the minimum verification needed to handle a request will be sought. Device-only records cannot be accessed or deleted remotely by the operator; use browser controls. Guardians may exercise applicable legal rights. Do not include unnecessary identifying information about children.

9. Protection and disposal

Support information is used only as needed to resolve requests. Emails and attachments no longer needed are removed using the email service's deletion process. Browser security settings govern local records. On a shared device, clear the game's site data after use.
